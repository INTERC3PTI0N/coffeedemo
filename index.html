01 — The Standard

999 is not a name. It is a threshold that almost nothing survives.

Three nines. Nine hundred and ninety-nine parts in a thousand. The assay mark struck into metal that has given up everything it was carrying — every trace, every convenience, every softening compromise.

We build to that figure and no other. What cannot hold the mark is returned to the fire.
