01 — The First Note

Order is not imposed on the dust. It is found, at one frequency, and nowhere else.

Scatter fine sand on a steel plate and draw a bow across its edge. At almost every pitch the grains simply jump and blur. Then you find the one frequency the plate wants, and in a second the chaos resolves into a figure so exact it looks drawn.

The grains have not become clever. They have been shaken off everywhere that moves and left only where the plate is still. What you are looking at is the shape of stillness. 999 is the mark for that condition in metal.
