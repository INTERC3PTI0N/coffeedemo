Altitude does something to a coffee cherry — it slows the sugar, tightens the bean, and leaves behind an acidity you can taste from the first sip to the last of the cup.

OUR SEASON
THE GROWING SEASON

Between October and February, when the highland nights drop to single digits, our growers walk the terraces and take only the cherries that have gone deep red. Nothing is stripped. Nothing is rushed. A picker will pass the same tree five times in a season.
